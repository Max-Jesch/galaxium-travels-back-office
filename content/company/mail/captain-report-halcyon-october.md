Operations,

Docked at Cape Meridian at 06:02, two minutes behind the block. Six travellers on the passage list, six aboard, all well. Outer C is off duty until the 19th of November.

Outbound on the 21st: seven of ten seats, on time, nothing to report. The float at turnover went to the business traveller in 2A, because 1A was empty; Maren supervised.

At Tombaugh: 48 hours on the ground. We slept aboard. The cooperative lent us their common room and their cook. Methane frost on the walkway both mornings; Maren walked every traveller to the crawler herself.

For the hangar: Idris logged the return temperature of the number 2 coolant loop climbing four degrees over the last six hours of the burn home. It stayed inside limits and the other two loops were steady. He would like the valve looked at before she flies another outer-system leg, and so would I.

One more thing, not for today. My arrival message from Tombaugh on the 22nd left at 06:20 and reached you at 10:50. For four and a half hours nobody on Earth knew that we had landed. That is how it is out there, and the travellers should hear it from us before they hear it from the silence.

Kaito Fujimori
Captain, Outer C
