To all carriers,

The registry moves to its new platform as announced in June. From Sunday 1 November, 00:00 UTC, to Thursday 5 November, 06:00 UTC, the registry accepts no new registrations.

A booking is registered before it is confirmed, so no carrier can issue a booking in this time. Price quotes that hold a seat are affected in the same way. Existing bookings, cancellations, boarding and passage lists are not affected.

The dates were agreed with the ports to fall outside the main booking weeks of the winter period.

Common Traveller Registry, run by the ports
