# Crew rest rules

These rules are not negotiable for scheduling reasons. Exceptions are signed by the Safety Officer and only for a named emergency.

| After | Minimum rest at destination |
| --- | --- |
| Earth to Luna shuttle (4 hours) | 10 hours |
| Inner-system leg (Mars, Venus) | 16 hours |
| Outer-system leg (Jupiter, Europa) | 26 hours |
| Pluto | 26 hours, and 48 hours before the next Pluto leg |

- A crew flies at most three outer-system legs in thirty days.
- Venus counts as an outer-system leg for rest purposes because of the radiation shielding procedure on approach. This is why the Venus turnaround cannot go below 26 hours with one crew.
- A relief crew stationed at the destination resets the turnaround: the ship may leave as soon as it is serviced.
