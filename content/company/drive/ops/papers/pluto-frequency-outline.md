# A second monthly Pluto departure: outline

Status: outline. First full draft due 9 November. Decision planned for the December leadership meeting.

## The question
Should we fly Earth to Pluto twice a month from March 2100?

## Options
| | Option | What it needs | Monthly cost |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| A | Stay at one scheduled departure a month | Nothing | 0 |
| B | Two departures with Halcyon back to back | Relief crew stationed at Tombaugh, second set of spares | 2,900,000 |
| C | Two departures with a leased second hull | A hull on the market, crew training | not yet known |

## What we know
- Halcyon is the only outer-system hull until Perihelion returns in February.
- Relief crew costing: ops/crew/relief-crew-tombaugh-costing.xlsx.
- The scheduled Pluto departure sold 7 of 10 seats in October and has 4 sold for November so far.

## What we do not know yet
- Which customers would commit in writing (Sales).
- Whether a hull can be leased, and at what price (Zainab).
- The break-even load for a second departure (Finance).
