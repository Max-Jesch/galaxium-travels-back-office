# A second monthly Pluto departure: outline

Status: outline. First full draft due 9 November. Decision planned for the December leadership meeting.

## The question
Should we fly Earth to Pluto twice a month from March 2100?

## Options
| | Option | What it needs | Monthly cost |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| A | Stay at one scheduled departure a month | Nothing | 0 |
| B | Two departures with Halcyon back to back | Relief crew stationed at Tombaugh, second set of spares | 2,900,000 |
| C | Two departures with a leased second hull | A hull on the market, crew training | not yet known |

The monthly cost is what the option adds on the ground. The flying of the second departure, out and home, comes on top in B and in C.

## What we know
- Halcyon is the only outer-system hull until Perihelion returns in February. The plan of record is that Perihelion then takes the two Europa legs and Halcyon flies Pluto only: ops/fleet/perihelion-refit-status.md.
- Why B needs a relief crew: a crew flies at most three outer-system legs in thirty days, and two departures are four. Before March there is a second reason: Halcyon has to be back for the leg out to Jupiter on the 9th, which leaves 30 hours at Tombaugh instead of 48.
- Relief crew costing: ops/crew/relief-crew-tombaugh-costing.xlsx.
- What a leg costs to fly, by distance: finance/analysis/cost-per-leg-2099.md.
- A second departure with seats on the public site needs a second public slot. We asked for one for March to August; the Slot Conference sits on 23 November (ops/schedule/slot-requests-2100-spring.md). Flown under contract it needs no public slot and sells no public seat.
- The scheduled Pluto departure sold 7 of 10 seats in October and has 4 sold for November so far.

## What we do not know yet
- Which customers would commit in writing (Sales).
- Whether a hull can be leased, and at what price (Zainab).
- The break-even load for a second departure (Finance).
