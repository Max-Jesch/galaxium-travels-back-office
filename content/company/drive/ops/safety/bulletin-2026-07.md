# Safety bulletin 2026-07: cabin baggage restraints, Kestrel class

**What happened.** Two reports in September of overhead restraint latches opening during the departure burn (Kestrel, rows 2 and 5). Bags stayed in place. Nobody was hurt.

**What we do now.**
1. Cabin crew check every latch by hand before departure, Kestrel and Meridian.
2. 48 replacement latches are ordered and due on 14 November. Kestrel first.
3. Until then, heavy items go under the seat.

**Who to tell.** If you see a latch that does not close with a click, report it in #safety or to Kwame directly. Reports are never held against the person who makes them.
