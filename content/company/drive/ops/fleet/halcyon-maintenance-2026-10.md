# Halcyon: unscheduled maintenance, 26 to 29 October 2026

**Finding.** Wear on the number 2 coolant valve, found at the routine inspection after the Pluto return of 23 October. Inside limits; projected to be outside limits after one more outer-system leg.

**Action.** Valve assembly replaced, system pressure tested for 12 hours, signed off 29 October 22:10.

**Effect on schedule.** None. No scheduled or charter departure was moved.

**Cost.** 640,000 credits (parts 410,000, labour 230,000). Charged to the maintenance reserve.

**Lesson.** With one outer-system hull there is no slack. A finding like this one week later would have cancelled a Pluto departure.
