# Perihelion: refit status, end of October 2099

At Hadley Yards, above Luna, since 1 June 2099. She receives the outer-system package and comes back as the second hull certified beyond Jupiter. This page follows the yard's monthly report; number 5 arrived on 30 October.

| Work | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Additional shielding for Europa's orbit | Done, August |
| Third coolant loop | Installed. Pressure test passed 22 October. |
| Cold-soak insulation and seals rated for Pluto | Insulation done. The seals for the hold doors came three weeks late and are fitted by 20 November. |
| Navigation and systems that do not depend on Earth | Bench tested. Installed in December. |
| Cold-soak trial, ten days | 5 to 15 January |
| Acceptance flight and certificate | First week of February |
| In service | By 20 February 2100 |

**Float.** Nine days between the planned date and the start of the March timetable. The late seals used twelve of the twenty-one we had.

**What she does when she is back.** From the March timetable she takes the two Europa legs and the leg out to Jupiter, with Outer A. Halcyon then flies Pluto only, with Outer C. Each ship is the other's spare, which we do not have today.

**If she is late.** Halcyon goes on flying Europa and Pluto alone, as now. Nothing is cancelled, and nothing can be added beyond Jupiter.

**Money.** The yard's price is $184 million. $150 million of it falls in 2099 and is in the forecast. Her lease has run all the time.

**Who watches it.** I am at the yard every Tuesday. Ragnhild's people do the acceptance checks in January.
