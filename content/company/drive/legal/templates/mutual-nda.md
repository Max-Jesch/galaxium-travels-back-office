# Mutual non-disclosure agreement (template)

Between Galaxium Travels and [counterparty].

1. **Purpose.** The parties wish to discuss [purpose] and may share confidential information for that purpose only.
2. **Confidential information** means anything marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including prices, schedules, customer data and contract terms.
3. **Duties.** Each party keeps the other's information secret, uses it only for the purpose, and shares it only with staff and advisers who need it.
4. **Exceptions.** Information that is public, already known, independently developed, or required by law to be disclosed.
5. **Term.** Two years from signature. Customer travel data stays confidential without limit.
6. **Return.** On request, information is returned or deleted within 30 days.
7. **Law.** Earth Commercial Code.

Do not change clauses 2, 5 or 6 without Legal.
