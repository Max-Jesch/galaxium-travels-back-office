# Pricing policy

Owner: Finance. Last reviewed 1 September 2026.

Every flight has a base price in credits. The fare a traveller pays is the base price of that flight multiplied by the seat class factor.

| Seat class | Factor |
| --- | --- |
| Economy | 1 |
| Business | 2.5 |
| Galaxium | 5 |

The fare shown on a price quote must equal the fare charged at booking. Any difference between a quote and the amount charged is a pricing incident and is reported to the Finance Director.

Base prices are set per route by Finance and Flight Operations and are listed in the booking system.
