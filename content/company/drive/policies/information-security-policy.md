# Information security policy (short version)

1. **Sign-in.** A second factor is required for mail, chat, the drive, the CRM and payroll. From 9 November 2026 without exception.
2. **Customer travel data** (names, itineraries, payment status) stays in the booking system, the ticket system and the CRM. Do not paste it into personal spreadsheets or outside tools.
3. **Exports.** An export of customer data to a file needs an owner and a reason, and is deleted when the reason has passed. Monthly finance exports live in finance/ in the drive and nowhere else.
4. **Requests from customers** for their own data go to legal@galaxium.example. The deadline is 30 days.
5. **Vendors** that process customer data sign our data processing addendum first.
6. **Report** anything odd to it@galaxium.example. Reporting your own mistake quickly is always the right thing.
