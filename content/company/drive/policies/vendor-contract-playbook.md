# Vendor contract playbook

Owner: Legal. Use this to review any vendor agreement before it goes to the General Counsel.

| Topic | Galaxium standard | Acceptable fallback | Escalate when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Term and renewal | Fixed term up to 24 months. No automatic renewal. | Automatic renewal for 12 months with 90 days' notice to stop it. | Automatic renewal longer than 12 months, or notice period over 90 days. |
| Liability cap | 12 months of fees. | 9 months of fees. | Anything below 9 months of fees. |
| Payment terms | Net 45 days. | Net 30 days. | Shorter than 30 days, or prepayment. |
| Service levels | Turnaround within 90 minutes, with service credits of at least 5% of the monthly fee per missed turnaround. | Service credits of at least 3%. | No service credits. |
| Termination for convenience | Galaxium may terminate with 60 days' notice. | 90 days' notice. | No right to terminate for convenience. |
| Price increases | Fixed for the term. | Annual increase capped at 3%. | Uncapped, or cap above 3%. |
| Governing law | Law of the Earth Common Jurisdiction. | Law of the Lunar Free Port. | Any other. |
| Data and safety incidents | Notice within 24 hours. | Notice within 48 hours. | Longer than 48 hours or no duty to notify. |

A review memo lists each deviation with the clause number, what the draft says, what the playbook says and a proposed change.
