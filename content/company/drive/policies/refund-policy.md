# Refund policy

Owner: Support. Applies to all cancellations made by the traveller.

What a traveller gets back depends on the number of days between the cancellation and the departure.

| Days before departure | Refund | Cancellation fee | Travel credit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 7 or more | 100% | 0% | 0% |
| 3 to under 7 | 50% | 25% | 25% |
| 1 to under 3 | 0% | 50% | 50% |
| Under 1 | 0% | 100% | 0% |

Percentages apply to the price paid. Exceptions need the approval of the Support Lead.
