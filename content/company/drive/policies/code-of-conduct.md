# Code of conduct

We carry people across the solar system. They trust us with their safety, their money and their plans.

- **Safety first.** Anyone may stop work for a safety reason. Nobody is blamed for a report made in good faith.
- **Tell the truth with numbers.** Say where a figure comes from. Say when you are not sure.
- **Customers get the same answer from all of us.** Policies are in the drive. If the policy is wrong, change the policy, do not make exceptions quietly.
- **Gifts.** Nothing above $10,000 from a vendor or customer. Declare what you accept.
- **Conflicts of interest.** Tell your manager before, not after.
- **Respect.** Three stations, many time zones. Assume good intent, write clearly, and do not schedule meetings at somebody else's night.
