# Discount policy for corporate contracts

| Discount from the rate card | Who approves |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 5% | Account executive |
| 5% to 10% | Head of Sales, with payment in advance by quarter |
| Above 10% | Head of Sales and Finance Director, in #deal-desk |

- Discounts apply to the base fare, never to fuel surcharges or port fees.
- Refund terms are not a discount lever. Ask in #deal-desk; the usual answer is travel credit, not cash.
- A price lock needs a signed commitment for the period it covers.
- The public booking site has no discounts. Its prices follow policies/pricing-policy.md.
