# When to escalate, and to whom

| Situation | Goes to | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Charge differs from what the customer was shown | Finance | Ticket in the support queue, tag "pricing", mail to finance@ if more than one case |
| Site error, double mail, hold behaving oddly | Engineering | #support-escalations and a ticket in the engineering queue |
| Assistance, baggage, anything at a station | Operations | #support-escalations, name the station |
| Request for a copy or deletion of personal data | Legal | Forward to legal@, tell the customer 30 days |
| Threat of legal action, press | Nadia, then Helena | Phone |
| Refund outside the policy | Nadia | Never promise first |

Always write the booking number on the ticket. A ticket without one costs somebody else ten minutes.
