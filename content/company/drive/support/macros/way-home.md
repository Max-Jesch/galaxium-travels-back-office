# Macro: how do I get home

Hello [name],

Your fare to [the Moon / Jupiter / Pluto] includes the way home. We fly back from there without seats for sale, and we take our own travellers with us.

- From the Moon: on our Luna shuttle, which leaves Tycho at 03:20 on ten mornings a month and is at Cape Meridian at 07:20.
- From Jupiter: on the 19th of the month, to Mars. From Mars the way to Earth is a departure on our site.
- From Pluto: on the 24th of the month, to Earth.

Tell me which date suits you and I will put you on the passage list. You will have a confirmation from me at the latest 48 hours before departure. There is nothing to pay.

[agent], Galaxium Support

**For agents:** the passage list is kept by operations control, not in the booking system. Write to opscontrol@ with the booking number of the outbound trip. From Mars, Venus and Europa there is no free way home: the traveller books the scheduled departure. A traveller who starts on Luna rides the shuttle free to reach any of our departures from Earth, and home again after.
