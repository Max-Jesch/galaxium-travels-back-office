# What a leg costs to fly, 2099

For Operations and Sales, who ask this every time a departure is added. From the ledger, January to September 2099. Rounded. The leases, insurance, crews and the office are not in here: they cost the same whether the leg is flown or not.

## Traffic control, per leg

System Traffic Control charges by distance band, not by the kilometre.

| Band | Legs in it | Charge per leg |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Local hop | Earth and Luna, Jupiter and Europa | 100,000 |
| Inner system | Mars, Venus, the Belt | 1,500,000 |
| Jupiter | Mars and Jupiter, Earth and Jupiter, Europa and Earth | 3,500,000 |
| Pluto | Earth and Pluto | 6,000,000 |

## Fuel

About $81,000 for every block hour, on average over the fleet. The Halcyon class burns more than that, the Kestrel class less.

## Ports, per turnaround

| Port | Paid to | Per turnaround |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Cape Meridian | Own staff, port dues | not charged by the turnaround |
| Tycho | Tycho Ground Services | 27,000 (380,000 a month for about fourteen) |
| Olympus Port | Olympus Port Authority | 900,000 |
| Aphrodite Station | Aphrodite Station Operations | 1,700,000 |
| Ganymede Gate | Galilean Port Services | 2,350,000 |
| Tombaugh Station | Tombaugh Station Cooperative | 5,200,000, with servicing |

Icebreaker charges nothing by the turnaround; we pay the Trust a share of the pad's upkeep once a year.

## Catering

Between $60,000 and $150,000 a departure, by the length of the leg.

## A worked example

Earth to Mars and back, as the contract rotation flies it: two inner-system legs (3,000,000), sixteen block hours of fuel (1,300,000), one turnaround at Olympus Port (900,000), catering twice (200,000). About $5.4 million. A full scheduled departure to Mars earns 18,500,000 one way.

## What this is not

It is not the cost of a seat. A leg we fly anyway, such as the way back from Jupiter, costs the same with nobody on board.

Sources: the ledger (traffic control, fuel), the invoice register (ports, catering), the operations control log (legs and block hours).
