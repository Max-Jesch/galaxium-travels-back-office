# Budget 2100: guidelines for departments

1. Start from your actuals through October, which Finance sends on 6 November.
2. Headcount: every new role needs a start month and a grade.
3. Any single item above $250,000 needs one sentence on what happens if we do not spend it.
4. Do not budget for a second outer-system hull or a relief crew. Fleet options are decided separately and will be added centrally.
5. Travel between stations is budgeted by the department that travels, at the internal rate card.

Timetable: first drafts 20 November, reviews 27 November, board 10 December.
