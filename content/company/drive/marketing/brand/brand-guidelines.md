# Brand guidelines (short)

**Voice.** Calm, exact, a little dry. We say what happens and when. We do not shout.

**Words we use.** Departure, traveller, seat hold.
**Words we avoid.** Blast off, adventure of a lifetime, guaranteed.

**Colours.** Deep night (#1B1A2E), dawn orange (#E8743B), station white (#F4F3FF).

**Type.** Headlines in the display face, everything else in the text face, never more than two sizes on one page.

**Decks.** Use templates/galaxium-deck-template.pptx. One message per slide, the source of every number in the footer.
