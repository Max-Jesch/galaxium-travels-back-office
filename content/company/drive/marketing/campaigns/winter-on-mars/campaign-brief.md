# Campaign brief: Winter on Mars

**Launch.** Tuesday 10 November 2099, 09:00.
**Runs until.** 28 February 2100.

**Idea.** Mars has a winter, and the dune lodges at Red Sands are at their best in it. We sell the trip, Red Sands sells the stay.

**Audience.** Leisure travellers from Earth and Luna who have flown with us once. Small groups.

**Offer.**
- Public site: no discount. Prices follow the pricing policy.
- Groups of eight or more through Sales: 10% off, departures 10 December to 28 February, at most 16 seats a month at that rate. Groups fly on the contract rotation (the 22nd out, the 26th back), not on the scheduled departure.

**Channels.** Customer newsletter, paid media on Earth and Luna, the Kepler Media films, the stand at the Interplanetary Travel Expo.

**What success looks like.** Earth to Mars and Moon to Mars departures in January and February sold out four weeks ahead.

**Not allowed to say.** "Guaranteed aurora." Legal has ruled it out, and so has astronomy.
