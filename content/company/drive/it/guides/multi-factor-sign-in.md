# Setting up a second factor

Mandatory from Monday 9 November 2099 for mail, chat, the drive and the CRM.

1. On your laptop, open your account page and choose "Security".
2. Choose "Add a device" and pick the authenticator app or a hardware key.
3. Scan the code with your phone. Older phone browsers loop on this step, so start on the laptop.
4. Type the six digits to confirm.
5. Print or save the recovery codes. Not on a sticky note.

Locked out? IT desk, third floor, or #help-it from a colleague's machine.
