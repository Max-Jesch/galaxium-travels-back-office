# VPN

You need the VPN for the warehouse, the admin pages of the booking system and the payroll system. Mail, chat and the drive work without it.

- The certificate was renewed on 22 October 2099 and is valid for a year.
- "Certificate not trusted": quit the client completely (menu bar, not the window) and start it again.
- Still not working: check that your laptop's clock is right. A clock that is minutes off breaks the certificate check.
