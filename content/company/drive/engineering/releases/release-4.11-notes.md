# Release notes 4.11

**4.11 (6 October)**
- Booking confirmations show the departure in the departure station's time zone.
- The admin list of bookings sorts by booking time.

**4.11.1 (13 October)**
- Connection pool to the payment provider raised from 20 to 60, timeout 10 seconds.

**4.11.2 (14 October)**
- The confirmation mail job no longer sends a second mail after a timeout.
