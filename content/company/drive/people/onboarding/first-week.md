# Your first week

**Day 1.** Laptop and badge from the IT desk, third floor. Lunch with your manager. Enrol a second factor for sign-in.
**Day 2.** Read the handbook, the code of conduct and ops/how-we-fly.md. The last one explains why "a booking" and "a deal" are different things.
**Day 3.** Security and privacy training (25 minutes).
**Day 4.** Sit with Support for an hour and listen to calls. Everyone does this, including the CEO.
**Day 5.** Tell your manager what surprised you.

Channels to join: #general, #announcements, your department's channel, #kudos.
