# Employee handbook

## Where we work
Earth HQ at Cape Meridian, the Luna Station office at Tycho, and the Mars office at Olympus Port. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your own station's time.

## How we work
- Write things down. Decisions live in the drive, not in a call.
- Chat for quick questions, mail for anything a customer or vendor sees, tickets for anything that needs an owner.
- The booking system is the record for bookings and quotes, the CRM for deals. A spreadsheet is a copy.

## Time off
25 days a year, plus the station holidays where you are based. Ask your manager two weeks ahead.
