# Battlecard: NovaTransit

**Who they are.** Low-cost carrier on Earth, Luna and Mars.

**Their pitch.** The lowest fare, and full refunds up to 24 hours before departure on their flexible tariff.

**Prices.** Below ours on Luna and Mars by 10% to 15%.

**Where we win.** Reliability, business class, anything beyond Mars.

**Where we lose.** Small budgets, and buyers for whom refund terms matter more than the fare: schools, universities, event travel.
