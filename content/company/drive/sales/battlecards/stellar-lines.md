# Battlecard: Stellar Lines

**Who they are.** Premium carrier with the widest outer-system network: Jupiter, Saturn stations, Pluto twice a month.

**Their pitch.** Reach. One contract for the whole system.

**Prices.** 10% to 20% above ours on comparable routes.

**Where we win.** Price on the inner routes, and service.

**Where we lose.** Customers who need Pluto more than once a month, or destinations we do not fly.

**Coming.** Two new outer-system hulls ordered for 2101.
