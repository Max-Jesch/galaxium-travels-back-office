# Battlecard: Orbitway

**Who they are.** The largest carrier on the inner routes. No outer-system service beyond Jupiter.

**Their pitch.** Frequency. Twice daily Earth to Mars from March 2100, weekly Jupiter. "Book the departure, not the fare."

**Prices.** About level with ours on Mars, higher on Jupiter.

**Where we win.** Pluto and Europa. Smaller ships and a quieter cabin. Seat holds for corporate bookers.

**Where we lose.** Any customer whose crews rotate on short cycles and need a departure every few days.

**What to ask the customer.** How often do your crews rotate, and what happens when one person misses a departure?
