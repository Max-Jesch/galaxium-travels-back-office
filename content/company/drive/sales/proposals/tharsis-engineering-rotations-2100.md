# Draft proposal: Tharsis Engineering, Ganymede site rotations 2100

Status: draft, due to the customer on 6 November.

**Request.** Four rotations Mars to Jupiter between February and November 2100: a site team of six out each time, and the team it relieves back to Mars.

**Offer.**
- Four contract departures on Aurora on fixed dates: February, May, August and November. The relieved team rides Aurora's way back.
- 24 economy seats at the rate card price of 2,500,000, way home included, less 5% for payment by quarter in advance: $57,000,000.
- Names: supplied at booking as required, with free name changes up to 72 hours before departure handled by Sales (cancel and rebook at no fee).

**Open questions.**
- Operations to confirm Aurora's dates and a crew. A contract departure to Jupiter and back is two outer-system legs. Outer B has one to spare in thirty days, so each of the four needs Outer C or a standby crew for one leg.
- Whether six seats on the scheduled Mars to Jupiter departure of the 17th would do instead. They would be bookings on the site, at the same fare, and no contract.
- Finance to confirm that the free name change is covered by the contract and not the public refund policy.
