# Corporate sales playbook

## Who we sell to
| Segment | Typical need | What decides |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mining and Resources | Crew rotations on fixed cycles | Departure days that match the shift calendar |
| Logistics | Crew changes at short notice | Frequency and flexibility |
| Research and Education | Seasons and field trips, fixed budgets | Reach (outer system), refund terms |
| Hospitality and Media | Guests and productions | Experience, dates around an event |

## Stages in the CRM
- **Prospect**: a named contact and a need.
- **Proposal**: a written offer has gone out.
- **Negotiation**: price or terms are being discussed, Legal may be involved.
- **Won / Lost**: closed. Write one note with the reason in the customer's words. Pick the loss reason from the list: Price, Schedule frequency, Outer-system coverage, Refund terms.

## Rules that save trouble
1. The CRM is the record. Forecast and commission are read from it.
2. A close date in the past on an open deal is a mistake. Fix it on Monday.
3. Discounts follow policies/discount-policy.md.
4. Never promise a departure that Operations has not confirmed.
