# Corporate sales playbook

## Who we sell to
| Segment | Typical need | What decides |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mining and Resources | Crew rotations on fixed cycles | Departure days that match the shift calendar |
| Logistics | Crew changes at short notice | Frequency and flexibility |
| Research and Education | Seasons and field trips, fixed budgets | Reach (outer system), refund terms |
| Hospitality and Media | Guests and productions | Experience, dates around an event |

## Stages in the CRM
- **Prospect**: a named contact and a need.
- **Proposal**: a written offer has gone out.
- **Negotiation**: price or terms are being discussed, Legal may be involved.
- **Won / Lost**: closed. Write one note with the reason in the customer's words. Pick the loss reason from the list: Price, Schedule frequency, Outer-system coverage, Refund terms.

## What a contract buys
- A block of seats or a whole ship on a contract departure: the Mars rotation (out on the 22nd, back on the 26th), the Belt call from Mars on the 1st, a Luna departure, or dates of the customer's own.
- Seats on the Luna shuttle, ten nights a month.
- A reserved seat on a way back we fly anyway: from Jupiter on the 19th, from Pluto on the 24th, from Luna on any shuttle.
- Never one of the ten public seats of a scheduled departure. A customer who wants those books on the site.
- Prices: sales/rate-card-2099.xlsx. Where we sell no way back to the public, the fare includes it.

## Rules that save trouble
1. The CRM is the record. Forecast and commission are read from it.
2. A close date in the past on an open deal is a mistake. Fix it on Monday.
3. Discounts follow policies/discount-policy.md.
4. Never promise a departure that Operations has not confirmed.

## Where the records are
- **Deals and accounts:** the CRM, with every closed deal since January 2098.
- **Signed contracts:** sales/contracts/contract-register.xlsx lists each one with its term and value. The charter orders are next to it.
- **Account plans:** sales/accounts, one per account, kept by the account owner or by customer success.
- **Customer stories:** sales/case-studies. Approved by the customer; they may be sent to anyone.
- **Trackers:** sales/pipeline-tracker-q*.xlsx, one a quarter. Working copies kept by hand, not the record.
- **Charter revenue by quarter:** finance/analysis/charter-revenue-by-quarter.xlsx, kept by Finance.
