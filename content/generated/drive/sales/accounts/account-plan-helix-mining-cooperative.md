# Account plan: Helix Mining Cooperative (A-101)

Mining and Resources · Mars · Key account · Customer since 2096 · Account owner: Dev Malhotra · Main contact: Oksana Petrenko, Travel Manager

**Who they are.** A cooperative of eleven mining sites in the Tharsis region of Mars. About 900 people on site, on fixed shifts of six months.

**How they travel.** Two whole ships a year: one out from Earth in the spring, one home in the autumn. From 2100 also a block on the monthly Mars rotation. Directors fly four times a year and buy those seats elsewhere.

**Health: good.** Our largest mining account. Quarterly review held on 12 October.

**What the customer says.** Oksana Petrenko, 12 October: happy with the rotation charter. Wants invoices by site instead of by traveller.
