# Ground truth: the quote mismatch

Generated by `scripts/build.py` from the snapshot. Do not edit by hand.

## The short answer

- Between 1 and 31 October 2026 the hold service issued **238 quotes**. **170** became bookings and 68 expired.
- Only **15 of 170** booked quotes match the amount charged.
- Customers were charged **more** than quoted on **121** bookings (total 251,600,000 credits) and **less** than quoted on **34** bookings (total 18,525,000 credits).
- Total quoted 193,850,000, total charged 426,925,000, net difference **233,075,000 credits** charged above quote.
- 16 of the 170 bookings were cancelled later. The figures above include them.

## The cause

The charged amounts follow the pricing policy (base price of the flight times 1, 2.5 or 5). The quotes do not:

- A quote ignores the route. It is 500,000 for economy and 2,500,000 for business, times 1.0, 1.1 or 1.2 depending on the flight id.
- The hold service does not know the galaxium class. All 21 galaxium quotes equal the economy quote for the same flight.
- So the charged amount is the correct one under the policy, and the quote is wrong. All three seat classes are affected.
